At the Propeller Advanced Workshop in Denver, H2 Enterprises and Dwire Earthmoving shared how they use drone surveys, DirtMate RTK, and the Propeller platform to run tighter data operations across large-scale earthwork, from 10,000-acre solar farms to complex quarry sites. Both sessions showed how giving field teams direct access to accurate, timestamped site data cuts rework, prevents disputes, and removes the wait for information that slows projects down.
