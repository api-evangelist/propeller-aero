360 Walkthroughs use an Insta360 X5 camera connected to the Propeller mobile app to capture navigable, GPS-anchored ground-level video of a construction site in minutes. Every frame carries a precise location, so the finished walkthrough appears directly on the site map in Propeller, navigable from any device, shareable via link, and permanently timestamped. The tool fills the visibility gap drones can't cover: trenches, culverts, pipe bedding, and pre-backfill as-built conditions.
